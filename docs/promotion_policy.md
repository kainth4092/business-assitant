# BigMart Product & Category Promotion Policy

## 1. Healthy Living Promotion Campaign
- **Target Products**: All items categorized as "Low Fat" in Dairy, Snacks, Baking Goods, and Frozen Foods.
- **Promotional Offer**: Automatic 15% discount off the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) applied at checkout.
- **Promotional Duration**: Runs continuously during the first 10 days of every calendar month across all tier outlets.

## 2. Category-Specific Bundle Discounts
- **Dairy & Breakfast Bundle**: Buy any Dairy item with MRP above \$100 and receive 20% off on associated Breakfast cereals or Breads.
- **Snack Foods & Soft Drinks Combo**: Purchase any 2 regular-sized Snack Foods and get 50% off a Soft Drink or Hard Drink of equal or lesser value.
- **Canned & Starchy Foods Stock-Up**: Buy 4 qualifying canned soup or vegetable units, get 1 free.

## 3. Outlet-Specific and Regional Promotions
- **Tier 3 Outlet Bulk Savings**: To support rural and remote communities, Tier 3 outlets (e.g., OUT010, OUT013, OUT018, OUT027) offer flat 10% volume discounts on multi-pack staple items (Household, Canned, Starchy Foods).
- **Supermarket Type 3 Weekend Flash Sales**: Supermarket Type 3 mega-stores run exclusive Friday-Sunday clearance events featuring up to 35% markdowns on seasonal and overstocked merchandise.

## 4. Coupon Stacking & Maximum Discount Thresholds
- Customers may combine one manufacturer coupon with one BigMart store promotion per item.
- The aggregate combined discount cannot exceed 40% of the item's original printed MRP.
- Cash-back promotions cannot exceed the total purchase price; no negative balances or cash payouts will be given for excess coupon values.
