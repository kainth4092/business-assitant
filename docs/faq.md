# BigMart Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Operating Hours & Store Locations
- **Supermarket Outlets (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3)**: Open Monday through Sunday from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM local time.
- **Grocery Stores**: Open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM; Sunday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Holiday schedules may vary; outlets are closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

## 2. BigMart Rewards Loyalty Program
- **Earning Points**: Members earn 1 reward point for every \$1 spent on eligible items across all outlet types.
- **Redemption**: 100 points can be redeemed for a \$5 discount on any checkout of \$25 or more.
- **Double Points Days**: Every Tuesday, all Low Fat dairy, fruits, vegetables, and health goods earn double loyalty points (2 points per \$1).

## 3. Delivery & Curbside Pickup Options
- **Curbside Pickup**: Free curbside pickup is available at all Supermarket Type 1, 2, and 3 locations on orders placed via our website or mobile app with a 2-hour preparation window.
- **Express Home Delivery**: Available within a 10-mile radius of participating supermarkets. Free delivery on orders exceeding \$50; a standard flat fee of \$4.99 applies to orders under \$50.
- Grocery store outlets do not currently offer home delivery services.

## 4. Accepted Payment Methods
- All major credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.
- Contactless mobile payments: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay.
- Government assistance: Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP / EBT cards) accepted on all qualified food items.
- BigMart gift cards and physical cash.
