# BigMart Corporate Return & Refund Policy

## 1. General Return Window
At BigMart, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. Customers may return eligible products within the designated return windows:
- **General Merchandise, Household Goods & Non-Perishables**: 30 calendar days from the date of purchase.
- **Perishable Food Items (Dairy, Meat, Seafood, Produce, Breads)**: 7 calendar days from the date of purchase due to freshness standards.
- **Packaged Snack Foods & Canned Goods**: 14 calendar days if unopened.

## 2. Condition Requirements
- All returned merchandise must be accompanied by the original register receipt, digital invoice, or loyalty card transaction record.
- Non-perishable items must be in their original packaging, unopened, and in resalable condition.
- If an item arrives expired, spoiled, or damaged at the time of purchase, customers are entitled to an immediate replacement or full refund within 48 hours of notification.

## 3. Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned or refunded once purchased:
- Opened health and personal hygiene products.
- Final clearance items marked with "AS-IS" or "Clearance Final Sale" tags.
- Perishable dairy or frozen items left unrefrigerated past 2 hours.

## 4. Refund Processing & Store Credit
- **Original Payment Method**: Refunds are issued to the original debit/credit card within 3-5 business days. Cash purchases are refunded in cash immediately at customer service.
- **Store Credit Incentive**: Customers choosing store credit receive a digital BigMart Gift Card loaded with the refund amount plus an extra 5% bonus courtesy credit.
- **Missing Receipt Policy**: Without a receipt, returns are refunded as store credit at the lowest promotional price in the preceding 60 days, subject to manager approval and valid government ID.
