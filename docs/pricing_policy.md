# BigMart Retail Pricing & Compliance Policy

## 1. Maximum Retail Price (MRP) Adherence
- Under corporate compliance rules, no BigMart outlet or franchisee is authorized to sell any product at a price exceeding the printed Maximum Retail Price (MRP).
- Scanning discrepancies at the register that result in an overcharge above MRP entitle the customer to receive the item free of charge (up to a \$20 value) under our Scan Accuracy Guarantee.

## 2. Competitor Price Match Guarantee
- BigMart outlets will match the verified advertised price of any local competitor located within a 5-mile driving radius.
- The item must be identical in brand, model, pack size, weight, and flavor.
- Customers must present a valid printed circular advertisement, competitor mobile app screenshot, or live digital flyer dated within the active promotion cycle.

## 3. Freshness Markdown Schedule for Perishables
To minimize food waste and optimize stock velocity, perishable goods undergo structured markdowns based on remaining shelf life:
- **14 Days to Expiration**: 10% markdown sticker applied to Dairy and Vacuum-sealed Meat.
- **7 Days to Expiration**: 25% markdown sticker applied.
- **2 Days to Expiration**: 50% "Quick Sale" markdown sticker applied.
- **Expiration Day**: Items are pulled from shelves at 6:00 PM for food bank donation or organic composting; sale of expired goods is strictly illegal.

## 4. Bulk and Wholesale Purchase Discounts
- Orders containing 20 or more units of an identical Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) qualify for an automatic 10% wholesale discount when purchased at Supermarket Type 3 hypermarket outlets.
- Commercial or institutional buyers must register their tax-exempt resale certificate at the customer service desk prior to ring-out.
