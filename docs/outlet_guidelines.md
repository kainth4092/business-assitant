# BigMart Outlet Classification & Operational Guidelines

## 1. Outlet Tier Classifications & Demographics
BigMart outlets are segmented into three operational tiers based on market density and population:
- **Tier 1 (Urban Metropolitan Centers)**: High-density downtown foot traffic (e.g., OUT049, OUT046, OUT019). Focuses on grab-and-go convenience, prepared meals, premium dairy, and express checkout aisles.
- **Tier 2 (Suburban Commercial Zones)**: Mid-size family neighborhoods (e.g., OUT035, OUT045, OUT017). Balanced stock of pantry staples, household cleaning, bulk goods, and weekly family groceries.
- **Tier 3 (Regional & Rural Hubs)**: Low-density communities with wide catchment areas (e.g., OUT010, OUT013, OUT018, OUT027). Heavy focus on bulk storage, durable canned goods, agricultural essentials, and high-capacity inventory.

## 2. Store Format Standards
- **Supermarket Type 1**: Standard full-service supermarket footprint (approx. 15,000 - 25,000 sq ft) offering fresh produce, bakery, and pharmacy.
- **Supermarket Type 2**: Modernized mid-tier supermarket (approx. 25,000 - 35,000 sq ft) equipped with expanded frozen foods and energy-efficient climate control.
- **Supermarket Type 3**: Hypermarket / Mega-center format (over 40,000 sq ft) acting as regional distribution nodes with full merchandise variety and wholesale pallet displays.
- **Grocery Store**: Compact neighborhood quick-stop format (under 5,000 sq ft) providing daily essentials without fresh meat counters.

## 3. Cold Chain Management & Storage Standards
- **Refrigerated Dairy & Meat**: Cold displays must maintain temperatures strictly between 1.0°C and 4.0°C (34°F - 39°F). Temperature logs must be audited twice daily.
- **Deep Freeze Units**: Frozen foods and ice cream must be stored at -18°C (0°F) or below. Any thaw event exceeding 30 minutes requires immediate quarantine of affected inventory.
- **Stock Rotation (FIFO)**: First-In, First-Out stocking is strictly mandatory across all shelves to eliminate expired inventory. Older stock must be placed at the front facing the customer.
